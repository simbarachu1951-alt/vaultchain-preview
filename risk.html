Risk Disclosure

Last updated: 11 August 2026

Important: VaultChain is not live for public trading. This is a general, pre-launch risk summary—not a complete disclosure document for a regulated product.

1. Loss of capital

Trading and investing involve risk. You may lose some or all of the capital you commit. Past performance, examples, illustrations, and projections do not guarantee future results.

2. Market and volatility risk

Prices can move rapidly due to market conditions, liquidity, macroeconomic events, technology failures, or other factors. Crypto-assets and other high-volatility instruments can experience significant and sudden losses.

3. Product and leverage risk

Different products carry different risks. Leverage, derivatives, margin, and short selling can magnify both gains and losses and may result in losses exceeding an initial outlay where legally permitted.

4. Liquidity and execution risk

You may be unable to buy or sell at an expected price or time. Network congestion, exchange outages, market disruption, and limited liquidity can affect execution and settlement.

5. Technology, fraud, and custody risk

Online services can be affected by cyberattacks, operational errors, unauthorized access, scams, protocol vulnerabilities, or third-party failures. Never share passwords, private keys, recovery phrases, or one-time codes.

6. Regulatory and tax risk

Laws, taxes, licensing requirements, asset classification, and access restrictions differ by jurisdiction and can change. You are responsible for understanding the rules applicable to you.

7. Suitability

Before using any future live service, consider your objectives, experience, financial situation, risk tolerance, and ability to absorb losses. Seek independent professional advice where appropriate.

8. No offer

This preview is not a solicitation, recommendation, or offer of a financial product. Any future offering will include final risk disclosures and customer terms before customers can transact.
