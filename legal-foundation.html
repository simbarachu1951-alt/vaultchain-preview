Legal Foundation & Service Status

Last updated: 11 August 2026

Current status: VaultChain is a public product preview only. It is not accepting customer funds or providing live trading, custody, brokerage, exchange, or payment services.

1. Current operating position

VaultChain’s intended primary market has not yet been specified for public launch. The project does not currently have an incorporated operating entity. Consequently, this website does not identify a legal entity, registered address, company number, regulator, licence number, or regulated trading service operator.

2. Regulatory statement

A financial authorisation has been declared for the intended project. It is not published on this site as a regulatory claim because no operating entity, jurisdiction, regulator, authorisation scope, or licence/registration reference has yet been identified and independently verified for VaultChain. Before any public claim of authorisation, the relevant licence must be legally linked to the incorporated operating entity and disclosed accurately.

3. Intended service scope

The intended product direction may include trading-related and custody-related workflows. These activities carry heightened legal, regulatory, operational, consumer-protection, financial-crime, safeguarding, and technology obligations. They are not currently available through VaultChain.

4. Conditions before launch

Before any public live service, customer funding, custody, or trading is enabled, VaultChain will need to complete and document, as applicable:

  • incorporation of the operating entity and publication of its legal details;
  • jurisdiction-specific licensing, registration, exemption analysis, or authorisation confirmation;
  • final customer terms, privacy notice, complaints procedure, risk disclosures, and financial-promotion approvals;
  • AML/CTF, sanctions, KYC, transaction-monitoring, recordkeeping, and reporting controls;
  • custody, safeguarding, security, incident-response, resilience, and third-party-risk arrangements;
  • tax, consumer-protection, data-protection, and marketing compliance review; and
  • independent legal and compliance approval for every jurisdiction served.

5. Geographic restrictions

No offer is made to persons in any jurisdiction. Access to a future live product, if launched, may be restricted based on location, legal eligibility, sanctions, identity verification, and risk assessment.
