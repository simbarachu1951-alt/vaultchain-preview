Privacy Notice

Last updated: 11 August 2026

Important: This is a pre-launch draft for the public preview. It must be reviewed and adapted for the jurisdictions in which VaultChain will operate before any live financial service launches.

1. Scope

This notice explains how VaultChain handles personal information submitted through its public preview and waitlist. It does not yet cover a live customer trading platform.

2. Information we collect

When you use the waitlist, we may receive your name, email address, and stated product interest. Standard web-hosting and security logs may also process technical information such as IP address, browser type, and request time.

3. How we use information

We use this information to respond to waitlist requests, provide requested product updates, protect the website, and improve the preview. We do not ask for financial account details, identity documents, or payment information through the public waitlist.

4. Legal basis and consent

Where required, we rely on your consent to send product updates. You may withdraw consent at any time by contacting us or using an unsubscribe method supplied in future communications.

5. Sharing and retention

We do not sell waitlist information. We may use service providers for hosting, email, security, and professional advice, subject to appropriate safeguards. We retain preview information only as long as needed for the purposes above, unless a longer period is required by law.

6. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or obtain a copy of your personal information, and to complain to a data-protection authority.

7. Security

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to a preview website, but no online transmission or storage system is completely secure. Do not send passwords, identity documents, bank details, wallet recovery phrases, or private keys to VaultChain through this site.

8. Contact

To make a privacy request, email simbarachu1951@gmail.com. Before launch, publish a legal entity name, registered address, data-protection contact, and jurisdiction-specific rights process.
