AML & KYC Framework

Last updated: 11 August 2026

Important: This describes intended pre-launch controls. VaultChain does not currently operate live customer onboarding, custody, trading, or deposit services through this public website.

1. Our intended approach

Before offering any live financial service, VaultChain intends to implement a risk-based anti-money-laundering, counter-terrorist-financing, sanctions, and customer-identification programme appropriate to each jurisdiction and product.

2. Customer due diligence

Where required before a live account is activated, customer due diligence may include identity verification, address verification, beneficial-owner checks, source-of-funds or source-of-wealth information, and enhanced due diligence for higher-risk cases.

3. Sanctions and screening

Prospective live customers and relevant transactions would be screened against applicable sanctions, politically exposed person, and adverse-media sources, subject to the laws and providers applicable to the service.

4. Monitoring and review

A live service would use proportionate monitoring for unusual or potentially suspicious activity, with escalation, investigation, recordkeeping, and reporting processes where legally required.

5. Restricted activity

VaultChain would not knowingly support activity involving money laundering, terrorist financing, sanctions evasion, fraud, identity theft, or other unlawful conduct. Accounts or transactions may be restricted where law, risk controls, or investigations require it.

6. Data and documents

Identity and due-diligence records would be collected only through a secure, approved onboarding process—not through the public preview or waitlist. Retention periods will be defined in final policies according to applicable law.
